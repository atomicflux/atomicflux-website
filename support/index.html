Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Atomic Flux?
No. Every feature works fully on-device without signing in. Signing in with Apple or Google only adds backup and sync across devices.
How do I back up or restore my habits?
Sign in with Apple or Google from Profile in the app. Your habits, streaks, and progress will sync automatically and restore on any device you sign into.
How do I delete my account and synced data?
Go to Profile → Connected Account → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your synced data from our server. Deleting the app itself only removes what's stored locally on your device.
Notifications aren't showing up — what do I check?
Make sure notifications are allowed for Atomic Flux in iOS Settings → Notifications, and check each habit's reminder time in the app under Notification Settings.
The "Habit Streak" widget isn't updating.
Widgets refresh periodically per iOS's own scheduling, not instantly. Try removing and re-adding the widget, or opening the app to force a refresh.
Where's your privacy policy?
Right here: atomicflux.app/privacy.
I found a bug or have a feature request.
Email info@atomicflux.app with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what happened, and your device/iOS version) — it helps a lot.
